How it works

Two moments it calls. Everything else, it doesn't.

GateKeeper only calls when you are inside your parking geofence and a vehicle signal is active. Walking home never opens the gate.

Immediate

Arriving by car

You drive into the geofence while Android already knows you're in a vehicle. The gate is called the moment you cross the line.

After 5 s check

Leaving the spot

You're parked inside and start driving, or your phone connects to Android Auto. GateKeeper waits 5 seconds, re-checks everything, then calls.

Never

Walking in

No vehicle signal, no call. Android Auto alone outside the geofence doesn't count either, and duplicate or late events never cause a second call.

Built to not misfire

Careful by default.

An automatic phone call is a real action. GateKeeper is designed so that a wrong call, a double call or a call from the wrong SIM doesn't happen quietly.

①

One call per arrival

Overlapping signals are merged. A call is recorded on disk before dialling, so a crash or restart never repeats it.

◎

The SIM you chose, or none

On dual-SIM phones it uses exactly the SIM you picked. If that SIM is missing it doesn't call, and tells you why.

⟲

Revalidated before dialling

Leaving the area, stopping, losing the signal, a revoked permission or a missing SIM during the wait cancels the call.

⚗

Test mode

Runs the exact same logic, with a simulator for every event, and never dials. Try it before trusting it.

☰

Full activity log

Every geofence crossing, vehicle change, Android Auto connection, trigger decision and call result, with timestamps.

✆

Tap-to-call fallback

If the phone blocks background calls, you get a notification to place the call with one tap. Nothing retries on its own.

Battery friendly

No always-on service. No GPS polling.

While you're parked at home nothing runs and there's no permanent notification. Android wakes GateKeeper only when something relevant happens.

  1. Google Play services reports geofence crossings and in-vehicle changes, even with the app closed.
  2. Your car's Bluetooth connection wakes the app when you get in. You pair it once through Android's companion-device dialog.
  3. A short check (up to 2.5 min) looks for Android Auto, waits, re-checks and calls. Then it stops.
Install

Five minutes to set up.

GateKeeper isn't on the Play Store, because Play's policies restrict automatic calling and background location. Signed APKs are published on GitHub Releases, and the app updates itself from there.

Download

Get the APK for your phone from the latest release. Most phones: arm64-v8a.

Grant

Location "Allow all the time", physical activity, phone, notifications. Each is explained first.

Configure

Gate number, SIM, parking spot on the map, radius. Pair your car's Bluetooth.

Test, then go

Start monitoring in test mode, check the activity log, then turn test mode off.

Honest limits

What Android doesn't guarantee

  • In-vehicle detection needs the car moving and can take 30–90 seconds.
  • Geofence alerts can lag by up to a couple of minutes, more in deep sleep.
  • Android Auto can only be checked while the app is briefly awake. Pairing your car's Bluetooth covers this.
  • Some phone makers (notably Xiaomi) block background calls unless you allow it in their settings.
  • GateKeeper is new. Test it on your phone before relying on it. All limitations →
Privacy & docs

Everything stays on your phone.

No account, no backend, no analytics, no ads. The only network requests are map tiles while you pick your spot, and an anonymous update check against GitHub.
